Fenwick Digital Art Prize — 2020 award record
19 November 2020

The 2020 Fenwick Digital Art Prize was awarded to Mara Vale for Folded Clock. The jury citation praised the installation's “precise material account of synchronization and drift.” The award record lists a £12,000 prize and a public talk held online on 3 December 2020.

The jury considered twelve shortlisted works. The record does not call the prize a lifetime achievement award or describe Vale as the first British recipient.
