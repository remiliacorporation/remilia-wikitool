Waiting rooms for machines
By Imani Sol, Circuit Review
8 May 2023

Sol argues that Vale's installations are strongest when their rules can be inferred from repeated observation. The essay compares Folded Clock with Rate Limit, shown at the Eastborough Media Biennial in April 2023. Rate Limit admitted one line of text to a projection every sixty seconds while discarding additional inputs.

Sol is critical of Borrowed Noon, writing that its generated phrases made the underlying sunrise tables harder rather than easier to understand. The essay nevertheless places the work within a broader group of British artists who translate software infrastructure into physical duration.

The essay reports critical interpretation, not a statement by Vale. It does not discuss Vale's private life.
