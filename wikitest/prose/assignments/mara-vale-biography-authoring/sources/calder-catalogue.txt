Inexact Measures: Mara Vale
Calder Museum exhibition catalogue
Exhibition 23 March–30 June 2024

Inexact Measures brought together five installations made between 2016 and 2024. The catalogue identifies Queue Weather (2016), Folded Clock (2019), Borrowed Noon (2021), Rate Limit (2023), and the new commission Counterpart (2024).

Curator Jana Rook describes Vale's practice as an investigation of “administrative time”: the schedules, delays, refresh intervals, and thresholds through which institutions organize attention. Rook distinguishes this interpretation from Vale's own narrower description of making system rules perceptible.

Counterpart used two receipt printers connected to separate public-data feeds. The printers produced parallel logs, but the exhibition did not claim that the feeds were complete records of public activity. The museum commissioned Counterpart and borrowed the four earlier works; it did not acquire Vale's archive or employ the artist.
