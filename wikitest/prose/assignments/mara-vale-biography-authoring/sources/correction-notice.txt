Correction: Common Signal artist list and dates
Eastborough Media Biennial
6 June 2023

An earlier web listing incorrectly dated Rate Limit to 2022. The commissioned installation was completed in 2023 and first presented at Common Signal from 14 April to 28 May 2023. Development tests took place in late 2022 but were not public exhibitions.

The corrected listing also restores Mara Vale's individual credit. A technical contractor, Parn Systems, built the input relay to Vale's specification; Parn Systems is not the artist or co-author of the installation.
