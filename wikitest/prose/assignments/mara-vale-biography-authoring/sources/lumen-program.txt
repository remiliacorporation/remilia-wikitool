Lumen Festival 2018 program
Published 30 August 2018

Interval Engine, by Mara Vale, ran in Gallery Three from 7–16 September 2018. The installation used browser timers and three mechanical shutters. Its program note says that small timing errors accumulated until the shutters no longer moved together.

The program lists Vale among twenty-nine participating artists. It calls Interval Engine a new festival commission. It does not describe the presentation as a solo exhibition and does not state that the festival discovered or launched Vale.
