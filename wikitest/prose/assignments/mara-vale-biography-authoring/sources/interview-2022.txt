Mara Vale in conversation with Esi Ward
Signal Practice podcast transcript
11 July 2022

Vale says Queue Weather began as a browser sketch in late 2015 and became the installation exhibited in 2016. Asked about Folded Clock, Vale says the first stable twelve-screen version was completed in February 2019; earlier tests used four screens.

Vale describes collaborating with different fabricators for different exhibitions and rejects the label of a permanent collective. Vale says the gallery handles sales but not the design or programming of the works.

When Ward asks why Vale cancelled a talk in 2020, Vale declines to discuss the reason and asks that the conversation remain about the work. No diagnosis or explanation is given.
