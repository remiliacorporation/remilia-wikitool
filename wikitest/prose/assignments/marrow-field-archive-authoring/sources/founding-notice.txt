Marrow Field Archive — public opening notice
4 November 2018

Marrow Field Archive opens its reading room today in the former East Parcel sorting office. The archive grew from a volunteer working group convened in September 2017 to preserve locally printed event calendars, handbills, newsletters, and community-radio schedules from the River Ward.

The opening collection contains approximately 2,100 physical objects. About 900 catalogue descriptions can be searched from terminals in the reading room. The notice calls the archive a project of the Marrow Field Trust, a nonprofit established in February 2018. It does not say that public collecting or public access began in 2017.

The reading room will initially open on two Saturdays each month. Material may be photographed unless a donor agreement or a fragile condition restricts handling.
