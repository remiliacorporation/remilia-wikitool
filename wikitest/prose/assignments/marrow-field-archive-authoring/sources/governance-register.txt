Charity register summary: Marrow Field Trust
Record updated 6 January 2025

Marrow Field Trust was incorporated on 12 February 2018. Its charitable purpose is the preservation of printed and recorded material concerning social and cultural activity in the River Ward and the provision of public access for research and education.

The Trust has five trustees. The register lists Samira Holt as chair and Teo Lin as archive director. Trustees approve budgets and collection policy; the archive director manages cataloguing and access decisions under that policy.

The register records grants from multiple public and charitable funders. It does not identify North Quay Museum as a trustee, parent body, owner, or controlling organization.
