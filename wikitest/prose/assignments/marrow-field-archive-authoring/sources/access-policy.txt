Marrow Field Archive — access and reproduction policy
Effective 1 October 2024

Catalogue descriptions may be viewed without registration. Reading-room visitors must book material in advance and show identification on arrival. Most holdings can be photographed for personal research.

Some donor agreements impose temporary closures, usually to protect personal correspondence or contact details. Closed descriptions remain visible when doing so does not itself disclose restricted information. Staff may also restrict fragile objects. The policy asks researchers to cite the archive and the catalogue identifier but does not transfer copyright or grant permission for commercial reuse.

The archive does not publish private donor addresses, private telephone numbers, or unverified allegations found in deposited papers.
