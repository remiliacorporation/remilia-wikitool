Marrow Field Trust — annual report for 2021
Published 18 March 2022

At 31 December 2021, the archive held about 4,800 physical objects grouped into 3,950 catalogue records. Staff had created 3,200 digital images, of which 2,740 were available through the public catalogue. One object can have several images, and one catalogue record can describe a folder containing multiple objects; the figures are therefore not interchangeable.

During 2021 the reading room received 286 visits. Volunteers completed a project to describe 412 community-radio schedules from 1996–2008. The report says that the archive's collecting depends on voluntary donations and should not be read as a complete record of cultural activity in the River Ward.

The Trust employed one part-time archivist and one part-time administrator. Twenty-three volunteers contributed description or scanning work.
