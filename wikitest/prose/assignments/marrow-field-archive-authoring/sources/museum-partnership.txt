North Quay Museum and Marrow Field Trust announce digitization partnership
North Quay Museum press office
16 August 2023

North Quay Museum awarded Marrow Field Trust a £38,000 equipment and training grant. The grant funded a camera stand, lighting, color targets, and six training sessions for archive volunteers. Museum conservators advised on handling brittle newsprint during the first three sessions.

The partnership runs from September 2023 through August 2025. The release says that catalogue records and images will continue to be published by Marrow Field Archive. Ownership and custody of the collection remain with Marrow Field Trust. The museum may link to public catalogue pages but receives no exclusive reproduction right.

The release calls the work a partnership. It does not call Marrow Field a museum department, museum collection, or jointly governed program.
