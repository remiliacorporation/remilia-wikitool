Marrow Field Trust — annual report for 2024
Published 27 February 2025

The public catalogue contained 7,460 records at the end of 2024. It linked to 5,930 publicly accessible images. The report does not give a current total for physical objects because a folder-level recataloguing project was still under way.

The reading room received 641 visits, and staff answered 188 remote reference requests. The archive began a two-year project to stabilize newspapers printed on acidic stock. It also published a revised description guide that records uncertainty rather than assigning an inferred date or creator.

The report describes a 2023 equipment grant from North Quay Museum as enabling higher-resolution capture. It states that Marrow Field Trust retained custody, cataloguing authority, and publication decisions. North Quay Museum did not acquire the collection.
