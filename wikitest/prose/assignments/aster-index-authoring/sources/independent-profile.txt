Document title: Preserving the hand-made web poster
Author: Inez Mar
Publisher: Digital Ephemera Review
Published: 18 June 2024
Fixture URL: https://fixtures.invalid/digital-ephemera-review/aster-index

Digital Ephemera Review describes Aster Index as a focused volunteer archive of posters circulated through small bulletin-board communities between 1998 and 2007. The review notes that the catalogue's community, year, and credited-designer fields make related posters easier to compare, while its uncertainty labels prevent undated material from being presented with false precision.

The article says the collection is useful as a record of informal graphic production, but cautions that it reflects voluntary contributions and is not a complete census of bulletin-board communities from the period.
