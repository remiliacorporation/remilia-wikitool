Aster Index is a volunteer-maintained digital archive established in 2022. It catalogues 480 scanned posters made for small bulletin-board communities active from 1998 through 2007. The archive was created to keep dispersed event notices, server advertisements, and community artwork available in one searchable collection.

Entries are organized by community, year, and credited designer when a credit appears on the poster. The maintainers do not assign a designer when the surviving image has no credit.
