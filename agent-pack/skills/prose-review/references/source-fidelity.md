# Source-fidelity review

## Review at claim granularity

Treat a claim as any proposition the reader is expected to believe, including implications created by juxtaposition, headings, captions, infobox values, and category placement. Split compound sentences into their component claims.

For each material claim, verify:

- the cited URL resolves to the named source rather than a domain root or unrelated article;
- the reviewer inspected the source body, not only metadata or a snippet;
- the passage entails the claim;
- actor, object, date, quantity, location, and version match;
- uncertainty, allegation, attribution, and scope are preserved;
- the citation is close enough that its coverage is not ambiguous.

## Citation laundering

Block prose that appears to inherit a source trail while severing the claim from its actual evidence. Common forms include:

- compressing a tertiary article with many references into one citation to that tertiary page;
- attaching several quotations or facts from different publications to one generic citation;
- citing a publisher home page instead of the work;
- copying citation metadata without inspecting the linked material;
- citing a local wiki page as if it were independent confirmation;
- upgrading a human source lead into an established fact.

## Drift in compression

Compare compressed prose for altered facts, not just missing nuance. Look specifically for:

- merged people or organizations;
- reversed direction of influence or attribution;
- approximate dates presented as exact;
- allegations presented as findings;
- a source's quoted characterization presented in the encyclopedia's voice;
- multiple episodes collapsed into one chronology;
- “associated with” becoming membership, authorship, or control.

## Close paraphrase and source voice

Compare sentence order, distinctive phrasing, example selection, and paragraph structure. A draft may be derivative even after synonym replacement. Rebuild from the claim map and multiple inspected sources rather than polishing a compressed source article.

## Source roles and weight

A primary source can establish its own content, release, or stated position. It usually cannot establish independent significance, broad reception, controversy, or the truth of self-description. A weak source can be accurate but still unsuitable for a consequential claim. Explain the mismatch rather than applying a universal source blacklist.

## Source-review adapter signals

The site adapter may match domains that deserve scrutiny, such as tertiary wikis or low-editorial-control databases. Treat a match as a deterministic routing signal. Decide appropriateness from the claim, subject, source role, and available alternatives. Record exceptions explicitly.
